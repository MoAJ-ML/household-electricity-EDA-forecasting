**Key Exploratory Data Analysis Findings:**

- **Trend & Seasonality:**
  - Daily and monthly plots reveal clear seasonality in household electricity demand.
  - Consumption tends to be higher during winter and summer months, indicating heating/cooling impact.
  - There is a visible weekly pattern, with some days showing slightly higher usage.

- **Peak and Off-Peak Periods:**
  - Hourly profile shows electricity demand peaks in the evening (typically 19:00–22:00), likely due to cooking and home activities.
  - Off-peak periods are late at night and early morning (00:00–06:00).

- **Weekday Patterns:**
  - Average power consumption by day of week is relatively stable, with minor increases on weekends.
  - Named days (Mon–Sun) are visualized for clarity.

- **Distribution & Variability:**
  - Monthly boxplots show greater variability during colder and hotter months.
  - Some months have higher median consumption, supporting the seasonality finding.

- **Anomaly Detection:**
  - Outlier points (using z-score > 3) were detected and visualized; these may correspond to unusual household events or data issues.

- **Usage Patterns (Clustering):**
  - KMeans clustering of hourly profiles identified high-usage and low-usage hours.
  - High-usage hours correspond to evening peaks; low-usage hours are overnight.
  - Actionable insight: shifting flexible appliance use to low-usage hours can reduce costs and grid strain.
